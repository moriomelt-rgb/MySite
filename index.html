Language toolkit · 2 minutes

Four steps to describe a place

  1. Think of the place. Do not say its name.
  2. Say where it is: near, beside, opposite.
  3. Say what it looks like: use two adjectives.
  4. Say what people do there.

Sentence frames

  • This is a ______ place.
  • It is near / beside / opposite ______.
  • It has ______ and ______.
  • Students go there to ______.
